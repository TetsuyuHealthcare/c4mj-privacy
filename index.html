C4MJ Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short

  • C4MJ is used by community care workers ("MicroJobbers") who take on short assignments for care organisations that subscribe to C4MJ.
  • We use your data to run your jobs, attendance, hours and payment records for your care organisation.
  • Your location is read only when you tap Check in or Check out, never in the background.
  • We do not sell your data, show advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Tetsuyu Healthcare Holdings Pte Ltd ("Tetsuyu", "we", "us") handles personal data in the C4MJ mobile app ("the app"), in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). It supplements our general Privacy Policy. Where the two differ, this policy applies to the C4MJ app.

2. Who is responsible for your data

You use C4MJ through a care organisation (for example a senior care centre, active ageing centre or nursing home) that has approved you as a MicroJobber. That organisation decides how your worker records are used and is responsible for them. Tetsuyu provides and operates the app and its servers, and processes your data on the organisation's behalf and in line with this policy.

3. What we collect and why

DataWhenWhy
Account and job information: your sign-in identity, and the jobs you take, register interest in or cancel When you use the appTo show you suitable jobs and manage your assignments
Attendance: check-in and check-out times; visit checklist answers for befriending visits When you check in or outTo record attendance and calculate hours and payment
Precise location Only at the moment you tap Check in or Check out, and only if you allow it To confirm you are at the job venue. It is stored with that attendance record. The app does not read your location at any other time or in the background.
Notification token: an identifier for your device's notifications After you sign in, if you allow notificationsTo send you job offers and shift updates
Usage analytics Only if your care organisation turns analytics on To understand how the app is used and improve it. A small set of events (for example "job taken" or "shift completed") is sent with ids, job type and hours, but never your name, address, contact details or any client information.

Your name, contact details and training records are entered by your care organisation, not by the app. The app displays them to you.

What we do not collect: contacts, photos, files, health data, motion or fitness data, background location, or advertising identifiers. If you use the camera to scan a server QR code on the sign-in screen, the image is read on your phone and is not stored or sent.

Demo mode: when the app runs on its built-in sample data, nothing you do is sent to any server.

4. Who we share it with

  • Your care organisation: everything in section 3 except analytics events, so it can manage your assignments, attendance and payment.
  • Service providers that help us run the app, only for that purpose and under confidentiality and data protection obligations:
    • server hosting for the C4MJ service;
    • Expo and Apple / Google push notification services, to deliver notifications;
    • Mixpanel, our analytics provider, only when your care organisation has turned analytics on.
  • Authorities, when required or allowed by law.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising. Some service providers may store data outside Singapore. When this happens we require them to protect it to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

5. Your choices

  • Location: you can refuse or turn off location access in your phone's settings. Your care organisation may then be unable to accept your check-in for jobs that need location confirmation.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings. The app still works.
  • Analytics: analytics is switched on or off by your care organisation. You can ask it, or us, about this.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you are a MicroJobber with your care organisation, and afterwards for as long as your organisation needs it for attendance, payment, audit or legal purposes. After that it is deleted or anonymised. Analytics events contain no name or contact details and are kept only as long as needed to understand how the app is used.

7. Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see or correct your data, to withdraw your consent, or to have your C4MJ account and data deleted. Contact your care organisation, or write to our Data Protection Officer at globalprivacy@tetsuyuhealthcare.com. We will respond within one month. Some records, such as attendance and payment history, may have to be kept for a period required by law or by your care organisation. If so, we will tell you.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and protected on our servers with access controls. Only people who need it to run the service or support your care organisation can access it.

9. Age

C4MJ is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or through your care organisation.
